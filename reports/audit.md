## Verdict

All 13 emotion dimensions are spread (population sd >= 0.08 on each). The colour field will vary.

## Honesty

This is a portrait of a dataset, not of humanity. MMTD and #nowplaying-RS are harvested from public Twitter posts in 2011-2013 and 2014-2018 respectively, so the listening they record is biased towards young, urban, anglophone, smartphone-owning people who tweeted what they were playing. Empty regions are mostly absence of that behaviour, not absence of music.
